Handwritten notes. Done right. Draw. Write. Annotate PDFs. Layers, pages and vector shapes. For students. For artists. For the office. Your toolbar. Your way. Export to PDF, PNG, SVG.

Tools

Pens, markers and shapes.

Everything you need to write and sketch – with full control over how it looks.

  • Pens & highlighters with adjustable width, round or square caps and opacity
  • Advanced color picker (RGB/Hex) and your own saved palettes
  • Lines, rectangles, circles and ovals, plus a built-in ruler
  • Text boxes with fonts and styles, and a lasso to move or delete anything
Smart gestures

Draw fast. Let Gerimo clean up.

Smart helpers keep your notes tidy without slowing you down.

  • Shape recognition turns freehand circles, squares and lines into exact vector shapes
  • Scribble to erase: just scribble over content to delete it
Layers & pages

Stay organized.

Keep notes, sketches and text on separate layers and build documents with as many pages as you need.

  • Layers you can select, show, hide or delete individually
  • Multi-page documents with a thumbnail overview and drag-to-sort
  • Export single pages or the whole document
Export

Take your work anywhere.

Export as PDF, PNG or SVG – or save a native .GERIMO file for backups and re-import.

  • Transparent background or keep your color and grid
  • Export all pages or only the ones you pick
